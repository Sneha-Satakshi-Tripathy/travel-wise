JAPAN · 8 DAYS · COUPLES

An 8-day Japan itinerary for couples

Japan can feel like a dozen trips at once. An enjoyable first visit usually comes from choosing a few connected bases, leaving room for food and neighbourhoods, and avoiding a new hotel every night.

Roamwise planning principle

Age is not a travel style. Use the group’s preferred pace, walking tolerance, interests and budget to shape the plan, and keep every day editable.

Days 1–3: Tokyo, with room to explore

Start with one neighbourhood each day instead of crossing the city repeatedly. Pair a landmark with a food stop, and keep the first day lighter if you arrive after a long flight.

Days 4–6: Kyoto’s quieter corners

Choose a small cluster of temples, gardens or historic streets each day. Popular sites are more pleasant early; build in café breaks and check the walking distances between stops.

Days 7–8: Osaka for food and an easy finish

Use Osaka for market food, evening streets and a flexible final day. If you prefer fewer hotel changes, make one city a day trip only after checking rail times and luggage logistics.

Make the route fit your energy

  • Pick a relaxed, balanced or packed pace.
  • Choose whether active walking is welcome or minimal walking is important.
  • Keep optional activities easy to skip when a day feels full.
