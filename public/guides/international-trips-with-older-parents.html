GROUP-AWARE TRAVEL · PRACTICAL GUIDE

Planning an international trip with older parents

The best trip for a 60-year-old couple might be full of museums and long walks—or a relaxed coastal stay. Age alone cannot tell you. A few explicit preference questions make planning more respectful and more useful.

Roamwise planning principle

Age is not a travel style. Use the group’s preferred pace, walking tolerance, interests and budget to shape the plan, and keep every day editable.

Start with pace and mobility

Ask how much walking feels comfortable, whether stairs are fine, how the group handles heat, and whether they prefer early starts or slower mornings. These answers are more useful than age alone.

Make transfer days realistic

Count hotel check-ins, luggage handling and station or airport transfers as part of the day. Prefer fewer bases when changing hotels would add stress, and confirm accessibility details directly with providers.

Offer a shared plan with optional branches

Choose one shared anchor activity, then provide nearby alternatives: a longer walk for some, a café or museum for others. Agree on meeting points and transport options in advance.

Keep safety information honest

Weather, visa rules, entry requirements, transport schedules and attraction access can change. Treat static guides as a starting point and verify important details through official sources before booking.
